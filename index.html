How we work

From "this is on fire" to "oh, it works."

A repeatable, opinionated path calibrated for speed, calibrated for risk, calibrated for "we'd like to keep our job."

01

Diagnose

Two-week audit. We map the system, surface the leaks, and put a number on the upside. You get a written brief, not a slide deck.

02

Design

Architecture, success metrics, and a fixed-scope plan. We agree the definition of done before any line is shipped (or any invoice is sent).

03

Build

Two-week sprints, weekly demos, shared Slack. The work is always live in a real environment never a slide, never a "trust me bro."

04

Operate

We hand over, train your team, and stay on-call. Or we run it for you. Either way, you own the system. (Yes, even the documentation.)
